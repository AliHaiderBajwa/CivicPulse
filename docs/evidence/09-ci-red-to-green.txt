How the pipeline blocks merges
------------------------------
Required status checks (all 7 contexts above) are configured on branch
protection for `dev` and `main`. A PR whose checks are red cannot be merged
until they pass; `gh pr merge` rejects it. Demonstrated live on the red
-> fix -> green cycle above and enforced from then on.
